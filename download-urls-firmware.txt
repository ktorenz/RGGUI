#   gun      <micro_usb|usb_c> <P1..P4> <url>
#   receiver <micro_usb|usb_c> <P1..P4> <url>
#   nuke     <micro_usb|usb_c> <url>
# usb_c = Pro Model (USB-C).  micro_usb = Old Model (Micro-USB).
